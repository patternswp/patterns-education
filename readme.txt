Patterns Education is a clean and versatile block-based WordPress theme designed for schools, colleges, online courses, and educational institutions. It provides all the tools you need to create a professional and engaging online presence for learning-focused platforms.

== Description ==

Patterns Education is a modern and functional Full Site Editing (FSE) WordPress theme, ideal for creating a professional online presence for schools, colleges, online courses, and educational institutions. Built with WordPress Full Site Editing (FSE), this theme makes it easy to customize headers, footers, templates, and global styles to suit your academic brand.

It includes pre-designed patterns and layouts tailored for showcasing courses, faculty profiles, testimonials, admission details, contact forms, and events. Its responsive design ensures your site looks polished and user-friendly on all devices.

Whether you're running a school, offering online classes, or managing an academic institution, Patterns Education provides a strong and flexible platform to engage students and promote learning.
